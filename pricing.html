Licence

One named person, personal use. Your copy carries your name and licence number. Licences cannot be shared, transferred or resold. Each family member who plans their own money needs their own licence.

System requirements

  • Windows app: a Windows PC with internet access for price updates.
  • Web app: a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • Workbook: Microsoft Excel (desktop) for the Excel-compatible workflow.
  • No account to create. Your plan is saved on your own device.

Updates policy

Your licence is for life: the version you have never stops working. It includes 12 months of updates and e-mail support from the date your licence is issued. After that, you can renew Updates & Support each year (optional) to keep getting new versions. Licences bought before 1 November 2026 include lifetime updates. When an update is ready, you are told how to get it. Back up your plan file before updating. Renewal: Updates & Support Renewal on Selar.

Support

E-mail support@legacyportfolioplanner.com or use the support form. Support covers installing, activating and using the planner. It does not include advice on what to invest in.
