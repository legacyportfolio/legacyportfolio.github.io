Features

Portfolio planning software built around your own records.

Each feature below explains what it does, why it matters and how Ada Okafor, our fictional example investor, uses it. The planner organises and calculates; every decision stays with you.

Planning Dashboard

What it does

Brings your current position, the three scenarios you defined, your asset mix against target, dividend income and true return together on one screen.

Why it matters

A plan you cannot see at a glance is a plan you stop checking. One screen makes a monthly check-in quick.

AO

Example with Ada. Once a month Ada does a five-minute check on the Dashboard. She sees her ₦20 million goal, where her portfolio stands, and that her top insight is an emergency fund below six months.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Goal Tracking

What it does

Sets your legacy target in today's money or future naira, tests it against your budget, and adds other goals such as a home, a car or school fees. A property affordability tool and a lump-sum schedule sit alongside.

Why it matters

Inflation changes what a naira target means over decades. Seeing both values keeps the goal honest.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada, 29, first typed ₦100 million by 45, then saw it would buy only about what ₦10.7 million buys today. She sets ₦20 million in today's money instead, and adds a six-month emergency fund and a laptop for 2028 as goals paid from separate savings, so they do not touch the long-term plan.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Portfolio Tracking

What it does

Records every holding, buy and sale. NGX share and ETF prices refresh in the app; other assets such as mutual funds, bonds or property are priced by you. Return is shown as a true money-weighted figure (XIRR), and broker statements can be imported.

Why it matters

Accurate records are the base of every review and of any handover. A true return figure stops large deposits from flattering the result.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada switches on her three holdings from the alphabetical NGX list, types one contract note and imports her other two buys from her broker statement. The list is for finding her own holdings, never a set of suggestions.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Dividend Monitoring

What it does

Logs dividends received, gross and net of withholding tax, and shows expected payments on a dividend calendar based on the records you keep.

Why it matters

Dividends are easy to miss when they arrive in different accounts. A complete log also helps a family trace payments later.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada started investing in October, so her income log is still empty. When her first dividend arrives she will record the net amount after withholding tax, and at year end see what her holdings paid her in total.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Asset Allocation Monitoring

What it does

Compares today's mix with the target weights you set, flags any holding, sector or asset class above the limit you chose, and lists the gaps in a Buy & trim worksheet. The worksheet never names a security to buy.

Why it matters

Portfolios drift as prices move. Checking the mix against your own written limits keeps risk where you intended it.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada's target mix is 60% blue-chip shares, 20% growth shares and 20% T-bills and bonds. All her money is in blue-chip shares today, so the screen shows her two under-weight classes. Her policy statement says to direct new money there first rather than sell; placing any trade is her own decision.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Scenario, Simulation and Stress Testing

What it does

Projects three scenarios from your own return and inflation assumptions, runs 500 simulated paths to show a range of outcomes, replays historical NGX All-Share years, applies four editable shocks, and compares different monthly amounts in a reality check.

Why it matters

A single straight line hides uncertainty. Ranges and shocks show how sensitive a plan is to the assumptions behind it. They are illustrations, not forecasts.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada runs a 2008-style crash: her all-shares portfolio falls about 35%, while her 60/20/20 target mix would fall about 30%. The reality check then shows how a 15% yearly step-up in her ₦150,000 a month changes the picture under the same assumptions.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Plan Health Scorecard

What it does

Runs 20 fixed checks across the whole plan, including emergency cover, savings rate, life cover, a written will with named beneficiaries, diversification and costs, and marks each OK, Review or Gap.

Why it matters

A long-term plan can fail for reasons that have nothing to do with markets, such as no emergency fund or no named beneficiary.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada's scorecard passes 8 of 20 checks. The biggest gap is emergency cover: 2.8 months against six, so she saves toward six months outside the portfolio as a separate goal.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Legacy Documentation (Investment Policy Statement)

What it does

Generates a written Investment Policy Statement from your plan: your goal, timeline, target mix, limits, review dates and selling rule, ready to print and sign.

Why it matters

Written rules make decisions calmer and give the people you trust a clear explanation of how you intended the portfolio to be run.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada signs her Investment Policy Statement and re-reads it at her yearly review on her birthday. Writing a will is still on her action plan.

SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY. Fictional investor; anonymised holdings.

Wealth Transfer and Estate Records

What it does

Tracks net worth and your legacy estate across assets, keeps scorecard records for your will, beneficiaries and insurance, and saves your whole plan to a backup file you can restore on another device.

Why it matters

A plan only becomes a legacy if someone can find and understand it. Keeping records current is the practical part of wealth transfer.

AO

Example with Ada. Ada logs her net worth, about ₦3.5 million including her pension, and keeps a backup file of her plan. A will with named beneficiaries is the next step on her action plan.

Rule-based, not AI. Every flag, check and insight in the planner comes from fixed, readable rules (for example, "a single holding is above the limit you set"). The planner does not use artificial intelligence to analyse markets, and it never selects investments.
