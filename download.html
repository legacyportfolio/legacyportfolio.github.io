Not sure which Mac you have?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it lists a Chip, choose Apple silicon. If it lists an Intel processor, choose Intel.

On a phone or tablet?

No download is needed. Open the web app in your browser.

Need a licence?

A licence key is needed to activate the planner, and a free demo is built in. Get a licence on Selar.

Windows: first-time note

If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac: first-time note

Unzip the file and drag the app to Applications. The first time, Control-click the app and choose Open. On macOS 15 or newer, you may need System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
