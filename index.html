The problem

Most family wealth plans live in someone's head.

Shares bought over many years, dividends paid into different accounts, a plan that was never written down. When the person who knows it all is not there, the family is left to piece it together.

01

Scattered records

Broker statements, registrar letters and dividend alerts sit in different inboxes, drawers and apps.

02

Forgotten plans

The goal set five years ago, and the reasons behind each decision, fade when they are never written down.

03

No documentation

Without a written policy, it is hard to review progress calmly or explain your approach to anyone else.

04

Hard to hand over

Families often struggle to trace holdings, unclaimed dividends and named beneficiaries when the time comes.

The solution

One organised record of your plan, your holdings and your decisions.

Legacy Portfolio Planner brings your goal, your portfolio records, your dividend history and your written investment policy into one place you control. It is organised the way a careful annual review runs, so you can update it in minutes and hand it on with confidence.

See every feature
  • You set the goal. Write your target in today's money and see what it means in future naira under the inflation rate you choose.
  • You record what you own. Holdings, trades and dividends in one ledger, with NGX share and ETF prices refreshed in the app.
  • You document your rules. Produce an Investment Policy Statement with your own limits, review dates and selling rule.
  • You keep it ready to pass on. Save a backup file and keep scorecard checks for your will, beneficiaries and insurance.
  • The tool never picks for you. It does not recommend any share or fund. Flags come from fixed rules you can read and change.
